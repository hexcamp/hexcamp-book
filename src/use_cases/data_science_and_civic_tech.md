# Data Science And Civic Tech

Because Hex.Camp communities can provide geo-bucketed storage that is controlled and managed by the communities themselves, it naturally is a great place to host datasets and websites that are relevant and important for those communities.

Local governments typically have a "Open Data" repositories that can easily be mapped to addressable locations. Important datasets can even be mirrored or archived into the Hex.Camp community for easy peer-to-peer syncing and long-term preservation.
